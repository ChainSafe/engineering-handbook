# Contributors

Authors and curators of the ChainSafe Engineering Handbook. This file is the central index of who contributed what and complements two other attribution layers: inline credits in each content file's header, and `CODEOWNERS` for review routing.

## How it works

The curatorial model: practices already exist across the org and get aggregated here with original authors credited. Anyone whose content lands in the handbook — even adapted, even partial — earns a credit.

- Add an entry when authoring new content, contributing material from an upstream source, or being added as a `CODEOWNER`.
- Format: GitHub handle, name, role, sections owned or contributed to. Free-form notes welcome.
- Removal is rare. Past contributors stay credited after they leave the org.
